# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Baseline community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, or anyone's health data, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions) and when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainer, [@Om-Kamath](https://github.com/Om-Kamath), through GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity, the maintainer may respond with a private warning, a request for a public apology, a temporary ban from interacting with the project, or a permanent ban.
